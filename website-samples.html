What a school website can do

Each school chooses the pages it needs. We can shape the design around the school’s identity and the information its community needs to reach.

01 / SCHOOL PRINCIPAL & LEADERSHIP

Share the school’s voice clearly.

  • Principal’s welcome, values, leadership and staff directory
  • Notices, term calendar, events and important dates
  • Policies, forms, downloads and official school information
  • Achievements, school news, facilities and approved photos
  • Clear enquiry, office contact and location details
02 / STUDENTS

Make everyday school information easier to find.

  • Class-wise resources, reading lists, assignments and projects
  • Timetables, exam dates, event calendars and quick links
  • Clubs, sports, activities, student work and achievements
  • Wellbeing, learning support and trusted contact information
  • Mobile-friendly pages and Hindi/English options where needed
03 / FAMILIES & TEACHERS

Keep the school community in sync.

  • Family updates, school routines, contacts and FAQs
  • Teacher introductions, class information and learning guidance
  • Admissions information and a straightforward enquiry path
  • Accessible layout, readable type and clear navigation
  • Searchable information and clear page sections that are easier to keep current
