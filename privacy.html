Privacy policy

Last updated: 4 November 2026

Markandey Pandey Pustak Mandir (MPPM) runs this information and school website showcase. This site is a static website and does not use a database or account system.

Information you choose to share

Enquiry links open WhatsApp with a message prepared for you. The site does not send that message or collect it on its own; you choose whether to send it in WhatsApp. WhatsApp then handles it under its own privacy terms. Do not include sensitive personal information in a public enquiry.

Browser storage

Some pages may use local browser storage to remember your language, appearance, or privacy-notice choice. This information stays in your browser and is not sent to MPPM. You can clear it through your browser settings.

Cookies and analytics

As of the date above, this site does not intentionally set analytics or advertising cookies and does not run an analytics service. External resources such as fonts, images, or WhatsApp may be requested from their providers, which may receive standard connection information. Their privacy practices are governed by their own policies.

School sample content and photos

School names, details, and images in design samples are illustrative. Sample photographs may be served by third-party image providers. A school’s published website should use information and photographs approved by that school.

Contact

For a privacy question, contact MPPM using the phone or WhatsApp links on the main website.

Changes

This policy may be updated when the website’s features change. The date at the top will be revised when it is updated.
